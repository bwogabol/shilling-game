
# National Budget Lab: Kenyan National Budget Simulation (MVP)

> An interactive strategic simulation game about allocating scarce national resources to resolve complex economic, humanitarian, and infrastructure challenges.

---

## Overview

You assume the role of **Cabinet Secretary for the National Treasury of the Republic of Kenya**. You are tasked with allocating a fictional national budget of **KSh 3.00 Trillion (3,000 Billion KSh)** across 10 vital sectors:

1. **Healthcare & Public Health** (Referral hospitals, primary care clinics, universal coverage)
2. **Education & TVET Skills** (Primary/secondary schools, vocational colleges, teacher hiring)
3. **Agriculture & Food Security** (Fertilizer subsidies, strategic food reserves, silos)
4. **Transport & Infrastructure** (Highways, rail corridors, ports, feeder roads)
5. **Internal Security & Defence** (Police modernization, border surveillance, anti-banditry)
6. **Water & Irrigation Schemes** (Multipurpose dams, boreholes, drip irrigation)
7. **Energy & Rural Electrification** (Geothermal drilling, national grid, solar mini-grids)
8. **Affordable Housing & Urbanization** (Slum upgrading, artisan employment, mortgages)
9. **Social Protection & Safety Nets** (Inua Jamii cash transfers, school meals)
10. **Governance & Devolution** (47 County Government transfers, KRA tax modernization, judiciary)

---

## Core Mechanics

- **Budget Conservation Law**: Exactly **KSh 3,000 Billion** must be allocated. The simulation cannot run if underallocated or overallocated.
- **Nonlinear Diminishing Returns & Starvation**: Pumping infinite money into one sector yields diminishing marginal returns; severe budget cuts (< 70% of baseline) trigger acute systemic crises and strikes.
- **Cross-Sector Synergies & Bottlenecks**:
  - *Food Sovereignty*: Water + Agriculture unlocked produces food security dividends and dampens inflation.
  - *Industrial Logistics Flywheel*: Infrastructure + Energy unlocks manufacturing; high infrastructure without energy creates underutilized white elephants.
  - *Human Capital Compounding*: Health + Education compound massively into long-term productivity and HDI over 5 and 10 years.
  - *Civic Trust*: Cuts to County Devolution or Social Protection trigger immediate public discontent and service shutdowns.
- **Three Time Horizons**:
  - **Year 1**: Immediate shockwaves & budget reallocations.
  - **Year 5**: Mid-term structural evolution & project commissioning.
  - **Year 10**: Generational legacy & debt sustainability verdict.
